Title of Study

Role of AI based Decision Support Systems (DSS) in Enabling Command and Control Decision-Making in High Intensity Conflict.

Purpose

This research investigates how artificial intelligence (AI) decision-support systems influence tactical/ operational decision-making at all levels of command authority in simulated operational environments through a vignette based experimental questionnaire.

Confidentiality

All responses are completely anonymous. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected.

Instructions

You will be presented with a series of simulated tactical / operational vignettes. Please answer based on your professional experience and judgment.
